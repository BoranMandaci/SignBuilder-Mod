# Sign Builder Mod 🎨

Sign Builder is a Minecraft Forge mod that allows players to seamlessly build, color, and illuminate custom 3D signs. Whether you are decorating a casino, setting up a shop, or just organizing your base, this mod provides highly detailed letters, a dynamic painting system, and glowing neon mechanics.

## ✨ Features
* **3D Letters (A-Z):** Highly detailed, custom-modeled letters that sit perfectly flat against the wall or floor.
* **The Paint Brush Tool:** A dedicated custom item to paint your letters dynamically.
* **Interactive Color Palette GUI:** Right-click in the air with the Paint Brush to open a smooth, 16-color visual selection menu.
* **Smart Block Tinting:** Change the color of your letters instantly with a simple right-click, without breaking the block or resetting its facing direction.
* **The Wrench Tool & Neon Mechanics:** A brand new custom tool! Right-click any letter with the Wrench to toggle its glowing (neon) state on or off, complete with satisfying metallic sound effects.
* **Immersive Feedback:** Custom vanilla-style dye and mechanical sounds for an intuitive user experience.
* **🌍 Global Localization:** Fully translated and supported in **English, Turkish, Russian, Spanish, Simplified Chinese, German, and French!**

## 🛠️ How to Use

**🎨 Painting Letters:**
1. Grab a **Paint Brush** from the Creative Menu.
2. Right-click in the air with the brush in your hand to open the Color Selection Menu.
3. Choose your desired color from the 16 available options.
4. Right-click on any SignBuilder letter in the world to instantly apply the color!

**🔧 Illuminating Letters:**
1. Grab the **Wrench** from the Creative Menu.
2. Right-click on any placed SignBuilder letter to toggle its internal lighting, instantly turning it into a glowing neon sign!
