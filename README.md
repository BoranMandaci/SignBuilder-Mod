# Sign Builder Mod 🎨

Sign Builder is a Minecraft Forge mod that allows players to seamlessly build, color, and illuminate custom 3D signs. Whether you are decorating a city, setting up a shop, or just organizing your base, this mod provides highly detailed characters, a dynamic painting system, and glowing neon mechanics.

## ✨ Features

*   **3D Letters & Numbers:** Highly detailed, custom-modeled letters (A-Z) and a complete set of numbers (0-9) that sit perfectly flat against the wall or floor. Visual models have been recently polished for a flawless look!
*   **The Paint Brush Tool & Advanced Palette:** A dedicated custom item to paint your blocks dynamically with an overhauled, robust color palette.
*   **Interactive Color Palette GUI:** Right-click in the air with the Paint Brush to open a smooth, visual selection menu for your color options.
*   **Smart Block Tinting:** Change the color of your letters and numbers instantly with a simple right-click, without breaking the block or resetting its facing direction.
*   **The Wrench Tool & Neon Mechanics:** A custom tool! Right-click any character with the Wrench to toggle its glowing (neon) state on or off, complete with satisfying metallic sound effects.
*   **Immersive Feedback:** Custom vanilla-style dye and mechanical sounds for an intuitive user experience.
*   **🌍 Global Localization:** Fully translated and supported in English, Turkish, Russian, Spanish, Simplified Chinese, German, French, and now **Italian**!

## 🛠️ How to Use

### 🎨 Painting Letters & Numbers:
1.  Grab a **Paint Brush** from the Creative Menu.
2.  Right-click in the air with the brush in your hand to open the Color Selection Menu.
3.  Choose your desired color from the available options.
4.  Right-click on any placed SignBuilder block in the world to instantly apply the color!

### 🔧 Illuminating:
1.  Grab the **Wrench** from the Creative Menu.
2.  Right-click on any placed SignBuilder block to toggle its internal lighting, instantly turning it into a glowing neon sign!
